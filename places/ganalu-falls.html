Ganalu Falls

On the Shimsha, a few kilometres before Shivanasamudra. Raw in the monsoon. Not a ticketed stop.

The drive

From Bengaluru the computed drive is about 1 hour 48 minutes and 98 kilometres, by car, on the road network — not a straight-line guess. Nearest city on the catalog is Malavalli. Maddur is the nearest station on record; that timing is indicative.

Good to know

  • A few kilometres before Shivanasamudra, on the Shimsha — not the packaged falls further along.
  • Aggressive in the monsoon. That is when it runs; it is also when it is least polite.
  • Uncommercialized. Do not expect stalls at the lip.

Same day or overnight

This is a same-day trip if you set the Return Clock to back by dark. The typical time on site in the catalog is six hours — leave room for the wet rock, not just the drive.
