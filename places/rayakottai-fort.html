Rayakottai Fort

Granite steps, ruined ramparts, the Bangalore–Salem line in the plains. No tree cover. Start early.

1h 12mdriving from Bengaluru
76 kmone way, computed
~7 hrstypical time on site

The drive

From Bengaluru: about 1 hour 12 minutes and 76 kilometres by car, computed. The town at the base has tea and snacks; you will not find them on the hill. The climb is about an hour on stone steps through old gateways. Best season in the catalog: November to February.

Good to know

  • Bikes and cars can be parked near the base of the hill in the town.
  • The path is exposed to the sun; start early morning to avoid the heat.
  • No entry fee or formal permit is required for the trek.
  • Basic food and water are available in Rayakottai town but not on the hill.

Same day or overnight

Same day, back by dark, if you start before the granite cooks. This is the one Summit Treks example we publish — the rest of that collection stays on the map.
